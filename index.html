Questions

Is Halo really free?

Yes. No trial, no account and no ads. If you enjoy it, you can leave a tip on Ko-fi to help keep it free.

Do I need to connect my Spotify account?

No. Halo reads what's playing straight from your computer, so there's nothing to log in to.

Where do the lyrics come from?

Halo looks up synced lyrics by the song's name from free lyrics libraries. Only the song name and artist are sent, nothing else.

Is Halo safe to download?

Yes. Both downloads were scanned on VirusTotal by over 65 antivirus engines, and none flagged them: Mac report · Windows report. Halo has no account, no ads and no tracking. To find lyrics, it only sends the song's name and artist.

Why does my computer warn me the first time?

Halo is new and made by an independent developer, so Apple and Microsoft don't recognize it yet. The steps above show how to open it. You only do this once.

Which computers does it work on?

Macs with an Apple chip (M1 or newer) on macOS 14.2 or later, and 64-bit Windows 10 (version 2004 or later) or Windows 11.

Does it slow my computer down?

No. Halo is built to be light: on a Mac the lyrics use only a few percent of the processor.
